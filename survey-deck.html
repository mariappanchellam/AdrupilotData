Slide 03 — Physical Operation

From Your Hand to Autonomous Survey

1
Stand near the factory (50–200 m away)
You hold the drone outside the building. Your GPS position becomes ArduPilot HOME (RTL return point). It does NOT need to be exactly at the factory centre.
2
QGC shows GPS OK + pre-arm pass
Wait for "EKF3 using GPS" and all pre-arm checks green. Minimum 45 s after power-on.
3
Set SURV_ENABLE = 1 in QGC
AP_AutoSurvey detects the 0→1 rising edge, builds the mission (TAKEOFF → 15 lanes → RTL), then requests AUTO mode.
4
Drone auto-arms and takes off from your hand
TAKEOFF command climbs to 50 m, then flies autonomously toward the factory centre coordinates.
TAKEOFF target → 12.87597° N, 79.86275° E @ 50 m
5
Lawnmower survey runs (≈ 15–20 min)
15 lanes × 290 m wide, 20 m spacing, 33 waypoints total. GCS shows "Mission: WP 2 / 33" counting up.
6
RTL — drone returns to YOUR position
Returns to HOME (your launch GPS fix, not factory centre). Lands, disarms. You swap battery here.
7
Wait 60 minutes → cycle repeats automatically
AP_AutoSurvey enters WAIT state. After 60 min it rebuilds the mission and auto-arms again. Runs indefinitely while SURV_ENABLE = 1.
Set SURV_ENABLE = 0 at any time to abort
